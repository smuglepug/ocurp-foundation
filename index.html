About Ocurp

Ocurp is dedicated to empowering young minds through education, sports, and community development initiatives. Founded in 2025, we believe that every child deserves access to quality education and opportunities to develop their full potential.

Our programs focus on providing educational resources, organizing community events, and creating platforms for young people to showcase their talents and skills. We work closely with schools, local businesses, and volunteers to make a lasting impact in communities across Nigeria.
